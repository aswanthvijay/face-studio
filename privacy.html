Google Play Store & User Data Compliance

Privacy Policy for Photovance AI & Face Studio

Effective Date: October 16, 2026

Application: Photovance AI (Package: com.photovance.app) & Face Studio Web Studio

Summary of Core Commitments:

  • No Permanent Storage of Face Photos: Reference photos are processed transiently in memory/ephemeral cache solely to execute the outfit transformation requested by you.
  • Automatic Ephemeral Cleanup: User uploaded images are automatically deleted from server memory within 24 hours.
  • Encrypted in Transit: All communication between mobile devices, Web Studio, and backend inference servers takes place over end-to-end encrypted TLS 1.3 / HTTPS connections.
  • No Biometric Sale or Marketing Exploitation: Your biometric landmarks and portrait photos are never sold, rented, or used for advertising targeting.

1. Data We Collect and Why

When you use Photovance AI to change an outfit or create an editorial scene:

  • User-Supplied Photos: Photos you select from your camera roll or camera are used exclusively to perform semantic dress segmentation and biometric facial identity alignment.
  • Text Prompts: Descriptions of fashion garments or styles you type are processed by our local diffusion pipeline to generate clothing textures.
  • Crash Diagnostics & Performance Data: Anonymized app crash logs and device metrics (such as Android OS version and device model) may be collected via Google Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose bugs and improve app stability.

2. Google Play Generative AI Policy Compliance

Photovance AI complies with Google Play’s Generative AI Policy:

  • Offensive Content Prevention: The application enforces strict negative prompt filtering to block the generation of sexually explicit imagery, non-consensual deepfakes, hate speech, and harassment.
  • User Reporting & Feedback: Users may report unwanted generation artifacts directly in the app.
  • Zero Stereotype Hallucinations: The engine uses physics-grounded melanin luminance matching rather than ethnic profiling keywords.

3. Monetization & Third-Party Advertising

Photovance AI integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to serve banner and interstitial advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, advertising IDs, and interaction data to serve relevant ads in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

You can request immediate deletion of any cached generation data at any time by contacting our support team or clearing your app storage in Android Settings.
